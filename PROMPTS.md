# PROMPTS.md - AI Productivity Assistant

## 1. Email Generation
**Prompt Used:** "Act as a professional workplace assistant. Write a polite, concise follow-up email about a delayed project. Include a subject line and signature."

## 2. Meeting Summarization
**Prompt Used:** "Summarize this meeting transcript into: Key Decisions, Action Items with owners, and Next Steps with deadlines."

## 3. Task Planning
**Prompt Used:** "Act as a productivity coach. Create a weekly task plan for a project manager with 3 priorities per day using time-blocking."

## 4. Research Assistance
**Prompt Used:** "Research top 5 AI tools for workplace productivity in 2026. Provide pros, cons, and pricing for each."

## 5. Chatbot Interaction
**Prompt Used:** "Act as a helpful HR and workplace policy chatbot. Answer questions concisely and professionally."

## Tools Evaluated
- ChatGPT (for email drafting)
- Gemini (for research)
- Claude (for summarization)

## Ethical Considerations
- No personal client data shared with AI
- Verified AI outputs before publishing
- Prompts designed to be inclusive and professional
